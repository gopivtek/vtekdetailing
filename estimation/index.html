Estimation

Our Steel Requirement Estimation Service provides accurate and detailed calculations of the total steel needed for your project. This service is essential in planning and budgeting, ensuring that you procure the right amount of steel while minimizing waste and avoiding costly overages.

Using advanced software and detailed drawings, we carefully analyze structural and miscellaneous components, including beams, columns, trusses, handrails, and more. Our estimations consider all necessary factors such as sizes, shapes, material grades, and connection types to provide a comprehensive report.

At Vtek Design and Detailing, we strive to deliver precise steel estimates that help streamline your procurement process and ensure efficient resource management. With our expertise, you can be confident that your project will stay on budget, with the exact amount of steel required for a successful build.
